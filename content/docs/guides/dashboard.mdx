---
title: Use the Dashboard
description: Manage devices, messages, contacts, and account security.
---

<Steps>

### Sign in

Register with email and password, or use Google sign-in when your deployment enables it.

### Create a device

Open **Devices**, select **Add device**, choose its platform, and give it a recognizable name. Dashboard saves the record and immediately opens **Connect**. Choose API Key, Put URL, or SMS registration; every platform supports all three methods. Platform is descriptive and can be changed later.

The Status column is the connection-management entry point. `Not connected` has no active credential, `Ready` is waiting for first Key use or SMS-code redemption, and `Connected` has completed that first use. Select any status to reopen Connect.

### Work with messages

Use **Messages** to browse or search by sender and content. Mark messages read or unread, delete records you no longer need, and use contacts to give frequent senders meaningful names.

### Maintain access

Review connected devices and public keys regularly. A Device Key does not expire automatically. Generate a replacement after accidental disclosure, or choose **Disconnect** to revoke the Key and any unused registration code. Disconnect preserves the device, messages, memo, and encryption-key bindings while resetting connection and sync timestamps.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Device Keys are credentials">Anyone with a Device Key can act as that source device. Do not paste it into tickets, chat, screenshots, logs, or source control.</Callout>
