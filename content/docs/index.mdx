SMS messages, verification codes, and device alerts should not be trapped on one phone, SIM card, or modem. PutSMS brings messages from the devices you control into one searchable, synchronized workspace.

## Why PutSMS

PutSMS began with a practical goal: make important SMS available where you need it without surrendering control of the devices, credentials, deployment, or private keys that protect it.

- Collect messages from iOS Shortcuts and App Intents, Android SmsForwarder, or Embedded hardware such as Air780EPM and ESP32-C2 + ML30.
- Read and search them from the Dashboard, iOS app, or browser extension.
- Keep working through local-first storage and incremental synchronization.
- Enable end-to-end encrypted uploads when every intended reader has a public key.
- Run the cloud components in your own Cloudflare account.

<Callout title="Encryption is capability-aware">
  iOS can require E2EE uploads and fail closed when no usable public key exists. Android webhooks and the current Air780EPM client send plaintext JSON over HTTPS. See the encryption guide before choosing a mode.
</Callout>
